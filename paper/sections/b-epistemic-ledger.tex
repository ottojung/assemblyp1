\section{Verification and claim scope}
\label{app:ledger}

The mathematical statements in this paper have different levels of support.
An automatically checked Lean theorem is not the same as a correspondence
between the formal model and a historical source, and an exact numerical
certificate is not by itself a proof of a general proposition.
The complete, declaration-by-declaration audit is available in
\texttt{docs/verification.md}; its longer earlier manuscript form is preserved
in \path{paper/technical-notes/}.

We distinguish five kinds of support:
(i) a kernel-checked Lean theorem with explicit hypotheses;
(ii) a mathematical proof in this paper not independently formalized;
(iii) a cited classical theorem;
(iv) an imported published reconstruction theorem; and
(v) a question left open.  When a Lean result is conditional on a published
theorem, we report \emph{both} qualifications rather than calling the
historical premise formally proved.

The mathematical claims in the main text always retain their hypotheses.
In particular, no abstract balanced flow is assumed to spell one genome,
and a source-specific genome or likelihood model is never identified with
