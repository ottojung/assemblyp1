\section{Verification and claim scope}
\label{app:ledger}

The mathematical assertions in this paper have different kinds of support.
Published reconstruction and combinatorial results are attributed to their
sources; original project-level conclusions are stated with their hypotheses;
Lean-checked results are distinguished from exact scripts and modeling
choices. The claim-to-proof index in \path{docs/verification.md} records
exact Lean namespaces, theorem hypotheses and independent check scripts,
without embedding development history in the manuscript.

The source's historical matched-string predicate and the manuscript's
sampled-placement predicate are not interchangeable. Likewise, oriented
reads, reverse-complement molecule classes, genuine bidirected flows and
full-overlap support proxies belong to distinct model domains.
These distinctions are mathematical assumptions rather than stages of
repository development.
