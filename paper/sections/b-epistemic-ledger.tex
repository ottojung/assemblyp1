\section{Epistemic ledger}
\label{app:ledger}

Every nontrivial claim in this paper belongs to one of a small number of
epistemic classes. This appendix consolidates them. The classes are:
\emph{source fact} (stated by a cited source), \emph{source-supported inference}
(a reading of a source that the source does not literally state),
\emph{project modeling choice} (introduced here, not in the literature),
\emph{mathematical result} (proved in the repository's notes),
\emph{kernel-checked} (checked in Lean),
\emph{verified computation} (exact-rational script), \emph{bounded computational
evidence} (search in a stated scope, not a proof), \emph{source gap} (the
sources do not determine it), and \emph{open}.

\subsection{Unresolved register}

We reproduce the project's open items so that the boundary of this paper is
explicit: (1) the referent of the 2016 sentence; (2) the read-type convention and
hence the equivalence; (3) the candidate-length convention; (4) the
maximizer-vs-uniqueness reading; (5) the $4^k$ vs.\ molecule-class index count in
MB09 Section~6.1; (6) the unretrieved publisher supplement; and (9) the population
model for molecule read types. The \textbf{final source-gap count} for the 2016
finite question is \textbf{7 unresolved items} (the canonical register
\texttt{docs/\allowbreak{}source-notes/\allowbreak{}finite-interpretation-universe-audit.\allowbreak{}md}, \S6, items
1--6 and~8; item~7, a repository-provenance gap, was closed). None is a matrix
row and none is used to decide one; the enumeration, and the three that bear
directly on the finite claim (the referent, the publisher supplement, and the
strand/equivalence convention), are in
\texttt{docs/\allowbreak{}source-notes/\allowbreak{}interpretation-matrix-217.\allowbreak{}md}, \S3.1.

Three items that used to appear on this list have been resolved and are recorded
in Table~\ref{tab:finite} instead. Item (7), the \emph{per-occurrence
same-length} Section~6.2 statement, is \emph{false}: the
$ATATACAC\to ATACACAC$ witness
(Theorem~\ref{thm:cex-peroccurrence}) has a per-occurrence-feasible truth and a
strictly better same-length competitor, and it needs the real four-letter DNA
alphabet — with a self-complementary base the searched scope has no same-length
beat at all. Item (8), the \emph{single-strand variable-length} Section~6.2
statement, is also \emph{false}: the oriented variable-length boundary witness beats
the truth as soon as $n>G$, under both objectives, and it is now kernel-checked
(\texttt{Oriented\allowbreak{}Variable\allowbreak{}Length\allowbreak{}Se62}) in infinite strict families, not merely
computed.

Two further items are explicitly \emph{not} settled and must not be absorbed into
the resolved class. The doubled-strand $2G$ convention of \cite{bresler2013} is a
project-level convention (not a source fact), is \emph{not a determinate source
row}, and is open in both directions; no bounded search is used as evidence, and
the known witness is inadmissible under the remap. The reading of the 2016
sentence as an unspecified general ML principle is not a determinate proposition,
because the source fixes no objective and no source argument settles it; its
three source-nameable members are each refuted, but the family reading itself
cannot be settled by a finite witness.

One leaf artifact is resolved as \emph{redundant} rather than pending, and it is
not a matrix row. Issue~\#216's general sample-multiplicity amplification
formalization is uncommitted, has no committed or board result, and does not
compile; it is deliberately not integrated because the amplification result is
already kernel-checked through \texttt{Oriented\allowbreak{}Variable\allowbreak{}Length\allowbreak{}Se62} and the
$AAATT\to AAAATT$ families, which are its canonical carriers.
Issue~\#219's same-length complete-spectrum fibre-count theorem is now fully
integrated: the note proves Theorems~1--3, an exact-arithmetic audit reproduces
them against brute force, and the Lean module
\texttt{Fibre\allowbreak{}Count\allowbreak{}Arithmetic.\allowbreak{}lean} kernel-checks the divisor-sum core (Möbius
inversion and the totient rearrangement). The BEST/Matrix-Tree graph content is
external, and the closest prior art (the $g=1$ multiplicity-one-edge corner) is
Shomorony et al.\ ISIT 2016, Appendix~C, Corollary~1, cited and not claimed. It
is a population-level counting result, not a finite-data reading of the 2016
sentence, so it resolves no row.

Item (7) deserves a label rather than a silent reclassification: the
per-occurrence requirement $d_D(w)\ge x(w)$ is \emph{not} Medvedev--Brudno's §6.2
rule, which states a per-vertex lower bound of $1$ and nothing stronger, so it is
a repository-added assumption surface (issue \#212). It is refuted because it is
a determinate mathematical question, not because the 2016 sentence asserts it.
With item (7) resolved, no determinate row of the matrix remains open; what is
left is a non-source convention and a non-determinate family, both flagged as
such in Table~\ref{tab:finite}.
