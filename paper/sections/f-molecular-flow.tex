\section{Reverse-complement molecule and bidirected-flow models}
\label{app:molecular-flow}

The oriented circular-read results in the main paper require neither
reverse-complement identification nor optimization over abstract
bidirected flows. This appendix records the separate, source-audited
molecular counterexamples and the full candidate-class comparison.

We now turn to the reverse-complement molecular reading of the MB09 flow.
Here read types are molecule classes, the truth and competitor must both be
admissible bidirected circuits, and the objective is the literal Section~6.1
product with external $N$. The mechanism is different from the sequence-level
witnesses: reverse complementarity \emph{creates} multiplicity that the
observation does not force, and support equality then permits reallocation.

\begin{theorem}[MB09 variable-length molecule counterexample]
\label{thm:cex-molecule}
Let $\Alphabet=\{A,T\}$ with involution $A\leftrightarrow T$, $L=3$, truth
$S=AAATT$ ($G=5$) with realized starts $0,1,4$, and competitor
$D=AAAATT$ ($|D|=6$). Then $R\in\Is$ holds; both $S$ and $D$ induce admissible
MB09 bidirected circuits; and with external $N=5$,
\[
  \frac{\Lik_{6.1}(D)}{\Lik_{6.1}(S)} = \frac{9}{8} > 1 .
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Sketch]
Under $A\leftrightarrow T$ the molecule classes of $S$ are
$d_S=(AAA:1,AAT:2,TAA:2)$ and the observed classes are
$x=(AAA:1,AAT:1,TAA:1)$; for $D$ they are $d_D=(AAA:2,AAT:2,TAA:2)$. Both
spectra have the same support as $x$. The ratio of the $AAA$ factor is
$[(2/5)(3/5)^2]/[(1/5)(4/5)^2]=9/8$, and the other two classes have equal
multiplicities, so they contribute factor $1$. Bidirected-circuit feasibility and
the $\Is$ certificate are checked by exact computation and by an explicit
bidirected graph.

\end{proof}

Allowing the lengths to be equal does not save the MB09 reading. The
smallest same-length witness is $S=AAATAT$, $D=AAAAAT$ ($G=6$), for which the
exact ratio is $3$ and the binomial ratio is $5$, with both genomes
MB09-feasible under the source's per-vertex lower bound.

\begin{theorem}[MB09 same-length counterexample under the per-occurrence
strengthening]
\label{thm:cex-peroccurrence}
Let $\Alphabet=\{A,C,G,T\}$ with the DNA involution $A\leftrightarrow T$,
$C\leftrightarrow G$ (no self-complementary base), $L=3$, truth
$S=ATATACAC$ ($G=8$) with realized starts $1,3,4,5,6,7$, and competitor
$D=ATACACAC$ ($|D|=|S|=8$), external $N=8$. Then $R\in\Is$ holds; both $S$ and
$D$ induce admissible MB09 bidirected circuits; both spectra have the same
support as the observation $x$; \emph{and} both are per-occurrence feasible,
$x(w)\le d_S(w)$ and $x(w)\le d_D(w)$ for every observed molecule class $w$ (the
tight coordinate is $ACA/TGT$, where $x=d_S=2$, while $x_{ATA/TAT}=1<3=d_S$, so
$n=6<G=8$ and the instance is not read-tiled). With both same-length objectives
the competitor is strictly better:
\[
  \frac{\Lik_E(D)}{\Lik_E(S)}=\frac{3}{2}>1,
  \qquad
  \frac{\Lik_{6.1}(D)}{\Lik_{6.1}(S)}=\frac{9}{5}>1 .
\]
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}[Sketch]
The molecule classes are $x=(ATA/TAT{:}1,\allowbreak TAC/GTA{:}1,\allowbreak ACA/TGT{:}2,\allowbreak CAC/GTG{:}1,\allowbreak CAT/ATG{:}1)$,
$d_S=(ATA/TAT{:}3,\allowbreak TAC/GTA{:}1,\allowbreak ACA/TGT{:}2,\allowbreak CAC/GTG{:}1,\allowbreak CAT/ATG{:}1)$ and
$d_D=(ATA/TAT{:}1,\allowbreak TAC/GTA{:}1,\allowbreak ACA/TGT{:}3,\allowbreak CAC/GTG{:}2,\allowbreak CAT/ATG{:}1)$; all three have the
same support, and $d_S,d_D\ge x$ coordinate-wise. Since $|D|=|S|$, the exact
factors are $3\cdot2$ against $1\cdot3$ on the redistributed coordinates, giving
$3/2$, and the binomial marginals give $9/5$. The $\Is$ certificate, the explicit
sixteen-edge bidirected overlap graph, the vacuous transitive reduction, the
signed-incidence balances and the vertex lower bound $1$ are all checked exactly,
for both genomes.
\kernelcheck{}
\end{proof}

The per-occurrence rule is what makes this witness necessary. The same-length
witness $AAATAT\to AAAAAT$ refutes the source's per-vertex reading, but its truth
is \emph{not} per-occurrence feasible ($d_S(AAA)=1<2=x_{AAA}$), so that
refutation does not transfer. On the binary alphabet $A\leftrightarrow T$ the
mechanism does not arise at all in the searched scope: with a self-complementary
base, a same-length per-occurrence instance has zero beats at
$(G,L)=(6,3),(7,3),(8,3)$ and $(6,4),(8,4)$, and the first beats appear at
$(G,L)=(8,3)$ on the real four-letter alphabet. The strengthening is a
repository-added surface, so this refutation settles a determinate mathematical
question rather than a reading of the 2016 sentence; it is reported because the
model comparison distinguishes determinate claims from source-interpretation questions.

\subsection{The source-faithful historical predicate, and two same-length witnesses}
\label{sec:finite:historical}

The finite witnesses above are stated for the placement-based realization
predicate $\Is$ of Definition~\ref{def:Is}, in which coverage and bridging are
evaluated at the latent sampled starts. Shomorony et al.'s own coverage and
bridging conditions are instead stated on the observed read \emph{strings},
matched at any genomic position, precisely because repeats let one read string
occur at many positions
\parencite[Supplement, \S6.4, Definitions~1--2]{shomorony2016}. The two predicates are
not the same, and the source's is the one the published question inherits from
the reconstruction theory. We record the source predicate, then show that the
same-length molecule failure survives under it. The compact form of the two
witnesses below is Counterexample~\ref{ex:W1W2} of Section~\ref{sec:open}.

\begin{proposition}[The two feasibility predicates are incomparable]
\label{prop:historical-source}
Let $I_s^{\mathrm{match}}$ be the source predicate of
Definition~\ref{def:historical-match} and let $\Is$ be the placement-based
predicate of Definition~\ref{def:Is}. Then neither implies the other. On
circular $AAAA$ with $L=3$ the single observed string $AAA$ matches at every
position and satisfies historical coverage, while its one realized placement at
start $0$ does not cover base $3$; conversely, on circular $ACGT$ with $L=2$ the
placements $0,2$ cover every base, yet historical coverage fails because $CG$
and $TA$ were never observed. The two finite models are therefore first-class
alternatives, and a result stated for one is not silently a result for the
other.
\statusline{\srcfact{} for the historical matching-string definitions
(Definition~\ref{def:historical-match})
\parencite[Supplement, \S6.4, Definitions~1--2]{shomorony2016}; the two
incomparability instances are \kernelcheck{} (Appendix~\ref{app:ledger}).}
\end{proposition}

\begin{theorem}[Historical same-length molecule counterexample $W_1$]
\label{thm:cex-historical-W1}
Let $\Alphabet=\{A,T\}$ with the DNA involution $A\leftrightarrow T$, $L=3$,
truth $S=AAATAT$ ($G=6$) sampled at the starts $(0,0,1,3,5)$, and competitor
$D=AAAAAT$ ($|D|=|S|=6$). Then
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),leftmargin=*]
  \item the source-faithful predicate holds at full strength,
        $S\in\Is^{\mathrm{match}}$: historical coverage, every literal
        three-occurrence triple repeat all-bridged, and every interleaved pair
        bridged;
  \item $S$ and $D$ are genuine MB09 bidirected DNA-molecule
        \emph{full-overlap} flows of the same length; and
  \item the competitor strictly wins under both source-named objectives, with
        external $N=6$ and the zero-count factors retained:
        \[
          \frac{\Lik_{\mathrm{ex}}(D;x)}{\Lik_{\mathrm{ex}}(S;x)}=3>1,
          \qquad
          \frac{\Lik_{\mathrm{bin}}(D;x)}{\Lik_{\mathrm{bin}}(S;x)}=5>1 .
        \]
\end{enumerate}
Concretely the molecule classes are
$x=(AAA{:}2,\ AAT{:}1,\ ATA/TAT{:}1,\ TAA{:}1)$,
$d_S=(AAA{:}1,\ AAT{:}1,\ ATA/TAT{:}3,\ TAA{:}1)$ and
$d_D=(AAA{:}3,\ AAT{:}1,\ ATA/TAT{:}1,\ TAA{:}1)$; all three share the same
support, so both candidates satisfy the source's per-vertex lower bound of $1$.
Hence, on the same-length molecule slice, the source's own read-string
bridging hypotheses do not make the truth a likelihood maximizer, and both the
maximizer and uniqueness schemas fail.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Historical same-length molecule counterexample $W_2$, per-occurrence]
\label{thm:cex-historical-W2}
Let $\Alphabet=\{A,C,G,T\}$ with $A\leftrightarrow T$, $C\leftrightarrow G$
(no self-complementary base), $L=3$, truth $S=ATATACAC$ ($G=8$) sampled at the
starts $(1,3,4,5,6,7)$, and competitor $D=ATACACAC$ ($|D|=|S|=8$). Then
$S\in\Is^{\mathrm{match}}$ at full strength; both $S$ and $D$ are genuine
MB09 bidirected full-overlap flows of the same length; \emph{and} both
satisfy the repository-added per-occurrence strengthening
$x(w)\le d_S(w)$, $x(w)\le d_D(w)$ for every observed molecule class $w$. With
external $N=8$,
\[
  \frac{\Lik_{\mathrm{ex}}(D;x)}{\Lik_{\mathrm{ex}}(S;x)}=\frac{3}{2}>1,
  \qquad
  \frac{\Lik_{\mathrm{bin}}(D;x)}{\Lik_{\mathrm{bin}}(S;x)}=\frac{9}{5}>1 .
\]
Thus the historical read-string predicate refutes the same-length molecule row
even under the strictly stronger per-occurrence surface, on the real
four-letter DNA alphabet.
\statusline{The per-occurrence strengthening is \emph{not} the source's
\S6.2 rule, which states only the per-vertex lower bound of $1$
(Remark~\ref{rem:surfaces}); the theorem reports a determinate mathematical
question, not a reading of the 2016 sentence.}
\end{theorem}

\begin{remark}[Five distinctions the historical witnesses turn on]
\label{rem:historical-distinctions}
The result above is easy to overstate, so we separate five pairs of notions that
the witnesses keep apart.
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*),leftmargin=*]
  \item \textbf{Observed string matching versus latent placements.} The source
        predicate $\Is^{\mathrm{match}}$ asks that an observed read
        \emph{string} match somewhere in a window; the placement-based $\Is$
        asks that a read placed at a sampled start do so. A placement certifies
        a match, never the converse, and the two are incomparable
        (Proposition~\ref{prop:historical-source}). Both models are retained;
        neither replaces the other.
  \item \textbf{Reverse-complement molecules versus oriented strings.} MB09
        reads are unordered reverse-complement \emph{molecule} classes
        \parencite[\S3.1,\S4.1]{medvedev2009}; Shomorony's windows are oriented
        \parencite[\S2]{shomorony2016}. The historical witnesses are molecular; the
        positive rigidity theorem is oriented, and the two read-type spaces must
        not be exchanged.
  \item \textbf{Full-overlap proxy versus literal bidirected graph.} The
        positive same-length theorem lives on the project's oriented
        full-overlap \emph{support proxy}; the historical witnesses are genuine
        literal \S6.2 bidirected molecule flows at the threshold
        $o_{\min}=L-1=2$, where circuits and consecutive-window spellings
        coincide. General-$o_{\min}<L-1$ flows, whose spelled words need not be
        consecutive windows, are not represented by either (Remark~\ref{rem:flow-domain}).
  \item \textbf{Exact multinomial versus fixed-$N$ binomial.} The exact
        objective uses the candidate-intrinsic length $N(D)$; the approximation
        uses an external genome size $N$ and per-type marginals. They are
        different objectives, and the witnesses report both: $(3,5)$ for $W_1$
        and $(3/2,9/5)$ for $W_2$.
  \item \textbf{Truth is a maximizer versus the maximizer is unique.} The
        historical witnesses are \emph{strict}, so they refute both schemas at
        once. The positive same-length row asserts more than a tie --- it makes
        the truth the unique maximizer up to cyclic rotation --- and the
        population theorem is the corresponding uniqueness statement at the
        population level.
\end{enumerate}
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[A parametric strengthening, stated but not used]
\label{rem:historical-param}
Adding $k$ further copies of the observed read $AAA$ at truth start $0$ leaves
historical coverage, matched-string bridging and both \S6.2 flow certificates
unchanged, and multiplies the two ratios of Theorem~\ref{thm:cex-historical-W1}
by $3^{k}$ and $5^{k}$, giving exact $3^{k+1}$ and binomial $5^{k+1}$ for every
$k\ge0$. The parametric extension follows by the same read-type
multiplication, but is not used as an independent
kernel-checked certificate. The base instance $k=0$ already settles the row.
\end{remark}

\paragraph{Reader-facing verdict.} Under the source's own \S6.4 read-string
predicate, the same-length molecule slice
is \emph{refuted}: the truth need not be a maximizer and the maximizer is not
unique. The positive rigidity theorem survives only on the oriented,
true-admissible, same-length, full-overlap slice, where it asserts uniqueness up
to rotation. What remains genuinely open is the referent of the 2016 sentence
and the general-$o_{\min}$ MB09 flow domain --- not the same-length
molecule row.

The finite landscape. ``False'' means a strict counterexample over the
stated class; the evidence level (kernel-checked, exact computation, or script)
is recorded in the corresponding theorem or status line. The positive
same-length rows assert maximizer and, on the truth-admissible genuine
candidate class, internally kernel-checked uniqueness up to cyclic rotation,
not merely that the truth ties. By Lemma~\ref{lem:negative-transfer} each same-length falsity
transfers to all circular candidates for that objective. The last two rows are
\emph{not} readings of the 2016 sentence: the $2G$ doubled-strand concatenation
belongs to a different paper and is the only determinate-looking row left open
(and it is open in both directions), while the disjoint-duplex row is a
repository modelling choice whose circle-by-circle reading is exactly $\Is(S)$.
The
per-occurrence strengthening in the row above them is likewise a repository-added
assumption surface rather than Medvedev--Brudno's MB09 rule; that row is a
\emph{determinate} question and is now refuted rather than left open. The two
\emph{source-historical} $I_s^{\mathrm{match}}$ rows use the source's own
read-string predicate of supplement \S6.4 rather than the placement-based $\Is$;
their historical coverage, literal all-triple and interleaved bridging,
\S6.2 bidirected-flow certificates and strict likelihood ratios are now
composed in Lean certificates. All
non-source rows are listed so that the claim of exhaustion is not overstated.


Table~\ref{tab:finite} makes the shape of the answer visible. The 2016 question
is not one theorem awaiting one proof: it is a family of statements indexed by
objective, strand convention, candidate class, and length convention, some true,
some false, and at least one open. On the single positive row, the truth is the
unique maximizer up to cyclic rotation, so that row settles the uniqueness schema
as well as the maximizer schema; the update from a bare tie to uniqueness is the
finite counterpart of the population result in Section~\ref{sec:population}. The
negative results also expose the
\emph{mechanism}, which the next section isolates: the exact objective scores
empirical read-type frequencies, and a competitor can improve its score by
reallocating multiplicity toward over-represented observed types while
respecting the bridging constraint on the realized reads.
