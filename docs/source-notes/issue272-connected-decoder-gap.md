# Issue #272: connected `I_s`-failing cases and the Not-So-Greedy decoder

_Status: 2026-10-23. Source-boundary audit and bounded computational evidence; no
claim about the output of the literal 2016 algorithm is proved._

## Question and scope

This note follows the algorithm-specific branch of
[`issue272-is-necessity.md`](issue272-is-necessity.md). It asks whether a
read-only-certain observation generated by a truth failing `I_s` can also be a
connected input to the literal Shomorony--Kim--Courtade--Tse (2016)
Not-So-Greedy decoder, and whether that decoder returns the truth there. These
are separate predicates: posterior certainty is a property of the whole
read-realizable support fibre; algorithm success is a property of a particular
graph construction, multiplicity modification, and Eulerian spelling rule.

## Source-defined graph boundary

The source construction is not the simple length-`(L-1)` de Bruijn graph used
for the adjacent `AAAB` illustration. The 2016 paper's Not-So-Greedy procedure
uses the observed distinct read strings as read-overlap-graph vertices, extends
overlaps greedily and then by the first disagreement, prunes spurious and
transitive edges, modifies edge multiplicities, and seeks a unique Eulerian
spelling. These steps and the uniqueness qualification are summarized in the
repository's source notes and in `docs/presentation-arc-265.md`; the guarantee
under `I_s` is attributed to Shomorony et al. (2016), Theorem 1. A connected
length-`(L-1)` overlap graph is therefore only a necessary preliminary filter
for a proposed example, not a formalization or simulation of that algorithm.

For `AAAB`, `L=3`, and read-type set `{AAA, ABA}`, the elementary directed
length-2 overlap graph has vertices `AAA`, `ABA` and no cross-edge: suffixes
`AA`, `BA` do not match the other vertex prefixes `AB`, `AA` in both required
directions. It is disconnected. This explains why the prior kernel-checked
read-only certainty witness does not settle the literal decoder case.

## Source theorem boundary

Keep the two external statements distinct. Shomorony et al. (2016), Theorem 1,
is the sufficient Not-So-Greedy reconstruction guarantee under its
information-feasibility hypothesis. Bresler, Bresler, and Tse (2013), Theorem 1,
is an information-theoretic ambiguity obstruction in its stated repeat setting
(including the all-copies-unbridged obstruction); it is not a characterization
of the output of the 2016 algorithm on every input. The gap between the weaker
obstruction and `I_s` sufficiency cannot establish algorithmic necessity or
sufficiency outside the published hypotheses.
